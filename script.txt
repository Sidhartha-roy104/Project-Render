Your energy is leaking, and the Gita says where. Verse 6.17: yoga ends sorrow for one who is balanced in eating, recreation, effort and sleep. Not extreme. Balanced. No burnout, no all-nighters, no endless scrolling. The Gita doesn't ask you to hide your energy from the world. It asks you to stop spending it carelessly.

## 2. 3D Illustrative Renders (Custom Generated with Gemini)

Per project guidelines: label-free 3D explainer visuals with zero embedded text, numbers, logos, or human faces. All are marked on-screen with an editorial `[ILLUSTRATIVE]` tag.

### Shot 1 — Keycap Press Mechanism
- **File**: `assets/images/shot1_keycap_press.jpg`
- **Description**: 3D isometric view of a mechanical keycap hovering over switch housing and spring.
- **License**: Custom project asset, illustrative.

### Shot 3 — Dense Wire Bundle (Tangled Chaos)
- **File**: `assets/images/shot3_tangled_wires.jpg`
- **Description**: Macro 3D render of tangled multi-colored wiring demonstrating 1-to-1 routing impracticality.
- **License**: Custom project asset, illustrative.

### Shot 5 — Orthogonal Circuit Intersection
- **File**: `assets/images/shot5_circuit_cross.jpg`
- **Description**: Clean 3D view of orthogonal gold traces meeting at a switch contact pin.
- **License**: Custom project asset, illustrative.

### Shot 7 — USB Cable Data Pulse
- **File**: `assets/images/shot7_usb_packet.jpg`
- **Description**: Conceptual 3D view of an electric blue data packet travelling through an exposed cable towards a monitor.
- **License**: Custom project asset, illustrative.
